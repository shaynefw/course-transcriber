TRANSCRIBE

        

          
          
          
        

        
On this Mac, “small” runs roughly 6–11× faster than real time. Lessons that already have a transcript are skipped, so you can stop and resume any time. The same model and language settings are used by Single files and Web videos.

        

        

      

    


    
    

      
TAKE SNAPSHOTS

        

          
        

        

          
          
          
          
        

        

        
Saves compressed JPEGs every few seconds while someone is talking, skips frames that look like the last saved one, and always grabs a frame at each [VISUAL] cue. Saved in snapshots/.

        
Needs transcripts. Starts 1 second before each [VISUAL] line and takes a frame at your “look every” rate for the burst length (overlaps are merged). Choose “every look” under KEEP to save frames even when nothing moved. Saved in snapshots-visual/.

        
Every [VISUAL] cue gets a full 1-frame-per-second burst (always saved, named _VISUAL) and everything else gets a background frame at your “look every” rate while someone is talking, skipping unchanged ones. Catches important moments the transcript didn’t flag. Saved in snapshots-combined/.

COPY-PASTE PROMPT FOR YOUR AI

WHAT THE AI WILL FIND

        
LOADING…

        

          
          
          
          
        

        
The AI keeps one small notes file per lesson in findings/ (named like P01-02-findings.md) so it doesn't have to re-read transcripts or clutter the chat. It creates them with python3 findings.py new "M02-04", and you can read them in the FILES tab (lesson → FINDINGS).
